1. Introduction and Scope

Welcome to WorkProof. This Privacy Policy governs your use of the WorkProof mobile application, our edge server endpoints, artificial intelligence (AI) processing pipelines, cloud backup integrations, and advertising networks.

We believe in radical transparency regarding how information is captured, transmitted, stored, and utilized. Please read this Privacy Policy with extreme care. By installing, opening, accessing, or using WorkProof—including snapping geotagged photos, submitting media to our AI Before & After service, viewing ads, purchasing in-app items, or connecting Google Drive—you acknowledge that you understand and explicitly consent to the collection, transmission, temporary Cloudflare storage, processing, and AI training practices detailed herein.

If you do not agree with this Privacy Policy in its entirety, you must discontinue all use of WorkProof and uninstall the Application immediately.

2. Infrastructure Architecture: Cloudflare Edge, Storage, and Data Lifecycle

2.1. Routing Through Cloudflare Infrastructure

WorkProof utilizes the cloud, CDN, and edge computing infrastructure provided by Cloudflare, Inc. (including Cloudflare Workers, Cloudflare R2 Object Storage, Cloudflare KV, Cloudflare AI Gateway, and Cloudflare Global Anycast Network) to route, secure, cache, inspect, and process network requests between your mobile device and our backend systems.

2.2. Processing Lifecycle, Non-Instantaneous Purge, and Periodic Deletion

NOTICE REGARDING DATA PROCESSING AND STORAGE LIFECYCLE:

When you utilize network-connected features in WorkProof—specifically the AI Before & After image creation:

  • Non-Instantaneous Purge: The photographs transmitted from your device to our Cloudflare backend are held temporarily during processing and are not purged in real-time the instant generation concludes; they may be cached to complete generation, provide verification, or support algorithmic evaluation.
  • Routine Deletion to Conserve Storage: WE ROUTINELY PURGE AND DELETE UPLOADED MEDIA AND TEMPORARY PROCESSING FILES FROM OUR CLOUDFLARE R2 STORAGE AT PERIODIC INTERVALS TO CONSERVE SERVER STORAGE CAPACITY AND MANAGE INFRASTRUCTURE COSTS.
  • No Permanent Archival on Our Servers: WorkProof does not provide long-term cloud hosting on its servers for your raw media. All completed Before & After comparison images and inspection reports should be stored on your local device or backed up to your personal Google Drive account.

3. Information We Collect and Process

3.1. Camera, Media, and Sensor Data

When you capture photos or record site inspections using WorkProof, the Application processes:

  • High-Resolution Photographs and Video: Full-resolution imagery captured via your device's camera hardware.
  • Embedded Geospatial Coordinates (GNSS/GPS): Latitude, longitude, altitude, horizontal and vertical accuracy estimates, velocity, and satellite constellation fix state.
  • Compass & Orientation Sensors: Azimuth heading degrees (magnetic and true north), pitch, roll, and device tilt.
  • Environmental & Device Metadata: Timestamp (local time and Coordinated Universal Time / UTC), battery level, network connectivity status (cellular carrier, Wi-Fi SSID status), device model, manufacturer, and Android OS build version.
  • Integrity & Anti-Tamper Telemetry: Flags indicating whether Android developer options are enabled, whether mock location providers are active, whether root/jailbreak binaries exist, and deterministic SHA-256 origin checksum hashes.

3.2. Artificial Intelligence (AI) Before & After Image Creation

WorkProof uses artificial intelligence strictly and exclusively to generate, align, match, and compose "Before & After" images (visual comparison pairs illustrating site progress, repairs, or project completion) and not for any other purpose:

  • Submitted Photos for Before & After Processing: When you use the Before & After feature, the site photographs you select (e.g., initial baseline photo and subsequent progress or completed photo) are transmitted through our Cloudflare edge backend to align perspective, match field-of-view angles, and composite the Before & After comparison.
  • Generated Outputs: The resulting composited, side-by-side, slider, or split-view Before & After comparison image and alignment metadata.
  • Strict Limitation of AI Scope: We do not employ artificial intelligence for structural defect detection, safety hazard flagging, automated text report generation, biometric scanning, or facial recognition.

3.3. Google Drive Login, File Access, and Storage Integration

WorkProof allows you to sign in with your Google Account to access files in Google Drive and to automatically or manually store images and PDF inspection reports created with our App.

A. Scopes and Data Accessed

When you sign in using Google Sign-In and authorize Google Drive, the App requests permissions under the following OAuth2 scopes:

  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file: Grants permission to create, upload, edit, organize, and store photos, videos, and PDF inspection reports generated by WorkProof in your dedicated backup folder (My Drive/WorkProof).
  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.readonly: Grants permission to read, list, view, and retrieve metadata and content of files and folders shared with you (such as team project folders connected by link or ID) so you can view, preview, and sync site evidence directly inside WorkProof.

B. Primary Uses of Google Drive Data

We access and process your Google Drive data strictly for two user-facing features:

  1. Cloud Backup & Storage: Uploading, organizing, and maintaining a secure cloud archive of all geotagged photos, site images, and PDF inspection reports created with the WorkProof app.
  2. File Access & Team Collaboration: Accessing, browsing, and previewing existing inspection files and shared folders from your team members within the WorkProof application.

C. Google API Services User Data Policy & Limited Use Disclosure

WorkProof's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements:

Specifically:

  • No Advertising Use: We never use, transfer, or disclose Google Drive user data or files to third-party advertising networks, data brokers, or information resellers.
  • No AI Model Training on Drive Data: Notwithstanding any general AI training provisions in this policy regarding public submissions, files, photos, and documents accessed from or stored to your Google Drive via Google APIs are NEVER used to train, retrain, or fine-tune artificial intelligence or machine learning models.
  • No Human Review: No human employees, contractors, or agents of WorkProof read or inspect your Google Drive files or photos unless you provide explicit consent for technical troubleshooting, it is necessary for security investigations, or it is required by applicable law.
  • Revocation & Deletion: You can revoke WorkProof's access to your Google Drive at any time via the in-app Sync Settings → Log Out button, or directly in your Google Account Third-Party Permissions. Revocation immediately stops all cloud access and synchronization.

4. Artificial Intelligence (AI) Models and Data Training Disclosures

CRITICAL DISCLOSURE REGARDING ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE MODEL TRAINING:
PHOTOS SUBMITTED TO CREATE BEFORE & AFTER IMAGES MAY BE USED TO TRAIN ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE MODELS.

4.1. Specific and Exclusive Use of AI: Before & After Image Creation

WorkProof utilizes artificial intelligence solely and exclusively to generate, align, match, and compose Before & After comparison images—allowing users to visually document job site progress, renovations, property conditions, and work completion. We do not use artificial intelligence for any other purpose.

4.2. Training, Tuning, and Algorithm Improvement

When you submit photos to our Cloudflare edge backend to create Before & After images, you acknowledge, agree, and explicitly consent that:

  1. Submitted Media Is Non-Ephemeral: The photos submitted for Before & After processing are routed through Cloudflare and may be persistently stored and cached across Cloudflare R2 object storage buckets and distributed edge nodes.
  2. AI Model Training: The artificial intelligence models, computer vision neural networks, and machine learning infrastructure (whether deployed on Cloudflare Workers AI or powered by third-party vision model providers) may or may not use submitted photographs, perspective alignments, and comparison pairs to train, retrain, fine-tune, evaluate, benchmark, and improve machine learning and computer vision algorithms.
  3. Indefinite Retention for Model Benchmarking: Submissions may be retained on Cloudflare servers and persistent storage systems indefinitely to validate alignment accuracy, evaluate perspective matching under varying lighting/angles, and improve automated image comparison models.

4.3. Waiver of Proprietary or Confidentiality Claims for AI Submissions

If you submit photos of classified facilities, proprietary architectural designs, sensitive industrial equipment, or private properties to the AI Before & After feature, you do so at your own exclusive risk. Because submitted media enters our non-ephemeral processing pipeline and may be utilized for machine learning model development, do not submit confidential, legally protected, or top-secret imagery to the AI Before & After feature if you do not want it stored on Cloudflare or potentially utilized for algorithmic training.

5. How We Use and Disclose Information

We process and disclose collected information for the following operational, commercial, and technical purposes:

  1. Delivering Core Application Functions: Capturing photos, burning in GPS/timestamp watermarks, compiling PDF inspection reports, and storing them locally on your device.
  2. Executing Non-Ephemeral Server Processing: Routing Before & After image alignment requests through Cloudflare Workers and persisting them on Cloudflare R2.
  3. Generating Before & After Images & Model Improvement: Processing site photo pairs to create aligned Before & After comparisons, and potentially utilizing such data to train and refine computer vision models as described in Section 4.
  4. Third-Party Advertising: Serving contextual, behavioral, or personalized advertisements to free-tier users via Google AdMob and participating ad exchanges.
  5. In-App Purchase Fulfillment: Validating purchases of Ad Removal and provisioning AI feature tokens via Google Play Billing.
  6. Cloud Storage Synchronization: Enabling two-way backup and synchronization with your designated Google Drive directories.
  7. Legal Compliance & Security: Preventing location-spoofing fraud, investigating malicious attacks on our edge infrastructure, protecting intellectual property, and complying with subpoenas or legal obligations.

8. Data Retention, Storage, and International Data Transfers

8.1. Local Device Retention

All photos, inspection drafts, and PDF files stored in your local app sandbox remain on your device until manually deleted by you or cleared when the Application is uninstalled.

8.2. Cloudflare Storage and Periodic Data Deletion

Data uploaded through Cloudflare for AI Before & After generation is temporarily stored and cached on Cloudflare R2 and edge systems during processing and subsequent algorithmic evaluation. To conserve storage capacity and manage server overhead, we routinely purge and delete uploaded images and temporary processing files from our Cloudflare R2 storage at periodic intervals. We do not provide permanent cloud archival for raw uploads on our servers.

8.3. International Data Transfers

Cloudflare and our AI processing providers maintain edge servers and data centers across the United States, Europe, Asia, and globally. By using WorkProof, you explicitly consent to the transfer, storage, and processing of your photos, metadata, and telemetry outside your country of residence, where data protection laws may differ from those in your home jurisdiction.

9. Security Safeguards

We implement administrative, technical, and physical security measures to safeguard data in transit and at rest:

  • All communications between the WorkProof client and Cloudflare edge endpoints are encrypted using modern Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.3 / HTTPS).
  • Cloudflare R2 object storage incorporates server-side encryption (AES-256).
  • Cryptographic origin hashes generated for photos utilize the standard SHA-256 hashing algorithm.

However, no mobile application, internet transmission, or cloud storage architecture is 100% immune from compromise. We cannot warrant, guarantee, or represent that unauthorized third parties will never be able to overcome our security safeguards.
