Questions

Where are my photos stored?

WorkProof keeps your captures on your device. You can connect your Google Drive account to back up or share selected work. Read the privacy policy for details.

What goes into a PDF report?

Select photos from a site visit and arrange them in the report editor. You can choose the page layout and include a declaration before exporting.

Can I keep separate records for each site?

Yes. Organize photos by project and site ID, and use the field map to revisit the location of a capture.
