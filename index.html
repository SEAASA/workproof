CRYPTOGRAPHIC INTEGRITY GUARANTEE

Tamper Detection That Exposes Modifications Instantly

In court or insurance arbitration, opposing parties can claim a photo was Photoshopped or metadata was modified. WorkProof solves this forever.

  • Pixel Binding Hash: The exact raw pixel byte stream is hashed using SHA-256 at the millisecond of capture and locked with hardware device keys.
  • In-App & Offline Verification: Load any photo into the Image Verifier. If even a single pixel or GPS coordinate was edited, it fails with a clear red tamper warning.
  • Scannable Micro-QR: Each photo carries a compact QR seal containing the cryptographic fingerprint, permitting verification without external databases.
